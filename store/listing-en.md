## Full description (max 4000)
```
Run your own chai tapri! Start with one small kettle on a street corner and grow it into the most popular chai stall in town.

TAP TO BREW
Tap the stall to brew hot chai and earn coins. Every tap counts, and customers keep walking in.

UPGRADE EVERYTHING
Buy better upgrades to earn more:
- Chulha: brew every cup faster
- Kettle: more cups in every batch
- Seats: more seats, more customers
- Helper: keeps the stall earning even while you are away
- Signboard: a bright board pulls customers in
- Radio: songs bring bigger tips and more VIP visits

Not sure what to upgrade? The game tells you whether brewing or customers are holding your income back.

GROW YOUR MENU
Start with cutting chai, then unlock Masala chai, Adrak chai and the legendary Kulhad chai. Better chai sells for more coins.

EARN WHILE YOU ARE AWAY
Hire a helper and your stall keeps earning for hours after you close the game. Come back to a "Welcome back" bonus!

DAILY REWARDS & VIP CUSTOMERS
Open the game every day for a 7-day reward calendar. Tap the VIP customers who drop by for a big tip.

OPTIONAL ADS, NEVER FORCED
Watch an optional ad to get a 2x income boost, a free helper, or to double a reward. There are no forced ads and no pop-ups while you play.

FEATURES
- Simple one-tap gameplay, perfect for short breaks
- Works offline, no login, no account
- English and Hindi (हिन्दी)
- Small download, light on battery and data
- Your progress is saved on your phone

A new stall is coming in the next update. Start brewing today!
```
